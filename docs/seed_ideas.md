- Order-book imbalance is a microstructure based quantitative signal derived from a limit order book which uses information about the instantaneous supply and demand to try to predict a short term future movement in the price of a security traded on an exchange. The goal is to inspect the current state of the order book and look for differences in supply (participants willing to sell a security) and demand (participants willing to buy a security) to determine whether they appear to be broadly matched. If an imbalance is detected we hypothesize that it is predictive of future movements in the price of the security.

- In a high frequency trading system designed to forecast the next move in the mid-price of a security we could use the immediate impulse of a trade happening in security S as a predictive signal for the next mid move in security S.

- Order flow imbalance is the idea of using a (recent) history of order book actions (e.g. quantity added at price X / level J) for security S to forecast short term forward returns of the same security S. Can we extend the base idea to add alpha which improves our ability to forecast returns?

- Sector mean reversion groups related stocks together in their industry sectors and hypothesises that on average all stocks in the sector will move together. We should short any stock which has outperformed the basket and go long stocks which have under-performed, doing our best to keep the portfolio market neutral.

- "Buy the rumour, sell the news" is the idea of buying a security on the expectation of good news before selling when the official announcement comes. The idea is to capitalise on the anticipation of an announcement which, if true, fundamentally reprices the stock.

- Related security momentum looks at intraday price momentum in stocks which are some way related to our target stock (maybe they are in the same industry sector, maybe they simply have a reasonable correlation with our target) and predicts that momentum in the independent security will be reflected in our target security. When we detect positive momentum in our related security we should be long our target and vice versa.
